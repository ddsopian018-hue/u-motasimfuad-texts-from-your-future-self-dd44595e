One text message from your future self, sent back to the present — 131 characters, no setup, just the message as it arrives:

> hey, it's you from 2036. stop saying 'i'll start monday' — monday just filed a restraining order. also, your plant died. let it go.

Why it wins: the voice is unmistakably a future self (a dated warning from 2036), the joke lands twice — procrastination escalated until Monday itself files a restraining order, then the drama is undercut by the smallest possible loss, a dead houseplant — and every character earns its place. The program gate-checks the line (single line, 1–200 characters, not whitespace-only) and writes exactly that line, with no trailing newline, to `entry.txt`.

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=./output node main.ts
```
